About George Sunia

George Sunia is a website dedicated to memes, images, and media about George Sun III (2011–), a living descendant to Sun Tzu, author of The Art of War.

What is a George Meme?

A George meme is a meme containing George Sun III's image and/or likeliness as a primary subject to convey a comedic thought.

It usually portrays Mr. Sun in a humorous way or recreating another popular meme.

The format started off as an inside joke between Oakton High School SCIOLY students, as George was a prominent figure in that community and George Memes frequently appeared as custom emojis in the school's SciOly Discord server. But we have a website now. See how much we've grown.
